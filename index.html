How It All Started

It began with a simple craving and a tiny kitchen in 2018. Frustrated by artificial powders and overly sweet syrups, our founders set out to recreate the authentic milk tea experience they remembered from childhood.

Months were spent sourcing premium loose-leaf teas from sustainable farms across Asia. Every batch of tapioca pearls was hand-cooked and boiled to achieve the perfect, elusive “Q” texture—that ideal balance of soft and chewy.

Meet Mithun,
Our Head Barista

“For me, making boba isn’t just following a recipe. It’s about rhythm, timing, and understanding how the ingredients interact. It’s a craft.”

Mithun joined Hashtag Boba on day one. With a background in culinary arts and a lifelong obsession with tea culture, he oversees our entire brewing process. From adjusting steep times based on the season’s tea harvest to ensuring every pearl is cooked to that perfect bounce, Mithun ensures our quality never wavers.
